# TheInterviewChair.com — pre-launch test checklist

Thank you for testing. Please try to break it. For anything that looks or sounds wrong, send: (1) a screenshot, (2) your device and browser, (3) exactly what you did, (4) the time. "It felt slow or confusing here" is useful too.

Start at https://www.theinterviewchair.com (use a private/incognito window for the first section, so you are a brand-new visitor).

## 1. First impression (2 minutes, as a stranger)
- [ ] Does the homepage make clear what this is within ten seconds?
- [ ] Does it feel safe and friendly? (Look for: "Private: only you see your interview", "no camera", "Make your mistakes here, not in the real interview".)
- [ ] Scroll the whole page. Anything cut off, overlapping or confusing?

## 2. Try it live (the free demo) — do it at least three times
- [ ] Use a different job each time (one ordinary, one unusual, one that is not a job, such as "driving theory test").
- [ ] Are the three questions different each time you run the same role?
- [ ] Answer once by voice and once by typing.
- [ ] The four buttons: **Repeat** (does it read the question again?), **Pause** (does it stop, and does Resume work?), **Tell Me The Answer** (does a model answer appear and get read out in a normal voice, not a robotic one?), **Pass** (does it move on?).
- [ ] Skip two of the three questions. Does the score drop a lot, and are the skipped questions listed as "Skipped"?
- [ ] On the results page: is the sign-up card right under the score? Press **Start my free interview**. Is your first name already filled in on the sign-up page, and is the role you practised ready on the setup screen afterwards?
- [ ] Listen for mispronunciations (especially "API"). Note any word that sounds wrong.

## 3. Different people and places
- [ ] Phone (iPhone and/or Android) and desktop, at least two browsers (Chrome plus one other).
- [ ] Try a non-English language (French, Spanish or any you know). Do the spoken lines, not just the questions, come out in that language?
- [ ] Try once on mobile data and once on Wi-Fi.

## 4. Account and full interview
- [ ] Register with a new email. Does the confirmation email arrive (check junk)?
- [ ] Start a normal full interview. Is the setup screen clear? Does the recording switch (green) explain who can see it?
- [ ] Finish it. Is the score report clear? Try "Tell Me The Answer" once mid-interview.
- [ ] Start a second interview: does your CV from the first one appear automatically ("Using your saved CV")? Try "Use a different CV" and "Remove it".
- [ ] **Day One Ready** (dashboard card): is the setup short, are all ten questions first-90-days scenarios, and is the report sensible?

## 5. Other pages
- [ ] CV Analyzer (free): upload a CV, read the result, press play on Amina's spoken feedback (no scrollbar showing on her card).
- [ ] Free Interview Questions page: does the sample question and example answer end cleanly (no cut-off words)?
- [ ] Help Centre inside the candidate portal, and the Privacy page: do they read clearly?
- [ ] theinterviewchair.com/book (the Founder's Diary): does it open in a new tab from the homepage links? Collapse a Part in the sidebar.

## 6. Break it
- [ ] Refresh in the middle of an interview. Go back. Close the tab and reopen.
- [ ] Type a very long answer, an empty answer, an answer in another language.
- [ ] Deny the microphone permission. Does it still work by typing?
- [ ] Anything that "looks off", even slightly. Small things count.

Thank you!
