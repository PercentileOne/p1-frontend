⭐ What FDEs Actually Do

FDEs are the engineers who sit closest to the money, the mission, and the customer's hardest problems. They are not "just coders".

Engineers Architects Product thinkers Problem-solvers Domain experts Operators Communicators Strategists

They are the people you send when the client is paying £10m+, the system is mission-critical, the data is sensitive, the stakes are high, the timeline is short, and the problem is unclear.

"Give me your hardest problem. I'll design the system. I'll build the system. I'll deploy the system. I'll fix the system. And I'll do it under pressure."
That's why they're paid £1M+. They are rare.
