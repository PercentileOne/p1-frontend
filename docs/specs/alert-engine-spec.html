Product Spec

InterviewMe Alert Engine

A two-sided notification system — recruiters watch for talent matching precise criteria; candidates see live demand for their role and are motivated to interview now.

Version 1.0 Author: Francis Cobbinah Date: 9 August 2026 Phase 1 Build
Overview
What the Alert Engine does

Two distinct alert types working together to create a virtuous loop: recruiters get notified the moment a matching candidate completes their interview; candidates see a live feed of active recruiter demand and are compelled to record right now.

Recruiter Talent Alerts
Set criteria once. Get notified automatically whenever a candidate completes an interview that matches — role, score threshold, location. No searching required.
Candidate Live Demand Feed
See how many recruiters are actively watching for your role and score band right now. A live, real-time signal that turns "I'll do it later" into "I need to record today."
Recruiter Portal
Talent Alert Setup

Recruiters access Alerts from a dedicated tab in their portal dashboard. They can create multiple alerts, pause or delete them, and see match history.

Recruiter alert flow
1
Create alert in Recruiter Portal → Alerts tab
Choose role, set score threshold (e.g. 90%+), optionally add location + radius. Name the alert. Save.
2
Alert goes live — system watches for matches
Every time a candidate completes an interview, the engine checks all active alerts. If criteria match, a notification is queued.
3
Match found — Toast + email fires
"A Senior React Developer in London just scored 94%. View their interview →" Links directly to the candidate's profile.
4
Recruiter reviews, likes, or inboxes candidate
One-click from the notification to watch the interview, like the candidate, or send a direct message.

Candidate Portal + Gate Page
Live Demand Feed

Candidates see aggregated, anonymous recruiter demand — how many active alerts exist for their role and score band. This appears on the gate page (to drive signups) and in the candidate portal (to motivate recording).

Candidate demand flow
1
Live Demand Feed visible on gate page (pre-login)
Anonymous — shows "62 recruiters watching Senior React Developers" without naming which companies. Drives signups.
2
Candidate portal → Alerts tab shows personalised demand
Once the candidate has a profile/role, they see demand specific to them: "14 recruiters are watching for your role scoring 85%+."
3
Candidate records their interview
The demand feed is the nudge. Once recorded, the system automatically checks them against all matching recruiter alerts.
4
Candidate gets notified when a recruiter matches them
"A recruiter just saved your profile from their Senior React Developer alert." Toast + email. Closes the loop.

Both Portals
The Alerts Tab

Both the recruiter and candidate portals get a dedicated Alerts tab. Same nav position, same design pattern — different content per side.

Recruiter Alerts tab contains
  • —List of active alerts with match counts
  • —"Create New Alert" button + form
  • —Match history — all triggered candidates
  • —Pause / Edit / Delete each alert
  • —Notification preference toggles
Candidate Alerts tab contains
  • —Live demand feed for their role
  • —How many recruiters match their profile
  • —History of recruiters who saved them
  • —"Record your interview" CTA if not yet done
  • —Notification preference toggles
Build Order
Phases

Build in sequence — each phase is shippable on its own.

Phase 1
Recruiter alert setup + matching engine
  • Alerts tab in recruiter portal with create / list / delete
  • Alert data model (role, score, location, radius, status)
  • Post-interview matching logic — check all active alerts on completion
  • In-app Toast notification on match
Phase 2
Email notifications + candidate feed
  • Email template: "Alert match — [Role] scored [X]% in [Location]"
  • Candidate portal → Alerts tab with personalised demand feed
  • Candidate Toast: "A recruiter just matched your profile"
  • Gate page live demand feed widget (anonymous, public)
Phase 3
Polish + growth mechanics
  • Alert match history with candidate mini-profiles in recruiter tab
  • Real-time demand feed updates (polling or WebSocket)
  • Weekly digest email: "Your alerts found X candidates this week"
  • Demand feed on gate page carousel as a dedicated slide
