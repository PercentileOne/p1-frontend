# TheInterviewChair.com: where we are

11 October 2026. Traffic and product facts only. Nothing here is paid for: all visits so far came from LinkedIn, TikTok, outreach, search and shared links.

## In one line

People are finding the platform, reading it and trying it. A quarter of real visitors interact, and phone visitors try the demo at nearly twice the rate of desktop. The work now is making the demo rock solid and turning "tried it" into "registered".

## What is working, and what we are fixing

- **Working:** people spend time on the page and reach the demo; real people are sharing the link; the UK engages most.
- **Fixing now:** demo reliability on Safari and iPhone (found and fixed this weekend); the steps between "Start" and the interview; and the step after the demo, where only 1 of 10 went on to register.
- **Next:** a campaign of 50 native posts starts on 11 October, each link tagged so we can trace a post through to a demo start, plus a daily "demo health" view in the admin.

Small numbers and a young product: read these as a direction, not a trend.
