- [ ] USPTO trademark search (most of your visitors are in the US)
- [ ] Companies House company-name search for "Interview Chair"
- [ ] A plain web search for businesses already trading as "Interview Chair" or "InterviewChair" (apps, courses, firms)
- [ ] Domains and handles for obvious variants (.co.uk, .net, .ai) and the same handle on X, Instagram and YouTube

What the results mean:
- The UK IPO does not refuse an application just because of someone else's earlier mark. It tells the owner of the earlier mark, and *they* can oppose. So the clearance search is the real protection, not the examiner.
- A name that is too descriptive of the service is what the examiner *can* refuse. "Interview chair" is allusive for AI interview practice, but a hot-seat or interview-seat phrase could be argued as descriptive. This is the main registrability risk.

## 6. Filing and what happens next

1. Apply online with the UK IPO and pay by card.
2. The application is examined, typically within a few weeks. Any objection on descriptiveness arrives here and can be answered.
3. If accepted, it is published and there is an **opposition period of about two months**. Watch your inbox and the post.
4. If nobody opposes, it is registered. Renewal is every 10 years.
5. Use **™** from now on. Do **not** use **®** until it is registered: using it before then is a criminal offence in the UK (Trade Marks Act 1994, s.95).

## 8. Honest notes and risks

- **The name is a little descriptive.** It is the main thing that could be pushed back on. A short, persuasive reply to an objection usually works, but there is no guarantee.
- **".com" adds nothing.** The protectable part is "The Interview Chair".
- **The avatars are not an ownable identity.** The library avatars from Spatius are shared with other customers, so the brand should rest on the name and logo, not on a face.
- **AI-generated logos have weak copyright.** Another reason the word mark matters more than the logo.
- **A marketing professional's name check is not a legal clearance.** Ask Chinaza which registers and countries she looked at, and when, and save what she sends you.
