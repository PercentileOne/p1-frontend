# Feed books: starter list (19 Oct 2026)

36 real books for the "Worth a read" strip on the Feed. Every ISBN below was looked up on Open Library on 19 Oct 2026: each is an English edition with a title and a cover picture. The admin page checks them again when you add them, so a book that has since lost its cover is refused with a plain message rather than shown broken.

## How to add them

1. Admin portal, then **Feed books**.
2. Paste the lines from the box below (everything between the fences) into the box.
3. Press **Check and add**. Each line shows a tick or a reason. Lines that fail stay in the box so you can fix them and press the button again.

The format is `ISBN | theme | why it is here | authors`. The authors part is there because Open Library's author lists can be messy (repeated names, translators). Edit any "why" line before pasting if you want it in your own voice. The strip tells people the note is ours.

No shop links and no affiliate code: a card shows Open Library's cover and opens the book's page on Open Library, where it can often be borrowed free.

## Things worth knowing

- The "why" lines are plain descriptions of what each book is about. None of them claims a result ("will get you the job") or quotes the book.
- Two ISBNs from my first search were the wrong book (a Young Adults adaptation of Frankl's book, and the *machine learning* system design book by Ali Aminian and Alex Xu). I caught both by checking every title against what I meant and replaced them: Frankl is now the standard Beacon Press edition, and *System Design Interview* is the second edition by Alex Xu and Sahn Lam.
- Some of these (the Nike, Frankl and Carnegie books, for example) exist in many editions with different covers. If a cover looks wrong or dated, pick another edition's ISBN from the book's Open Library page and re-add it.
- Duplicates are refused: pasting the same ISBN twice is safe.
