# P1 North Star

## The Mission

**To help 1 million people close the gap between who they are and who they intend to become.**

Not to make people feel productive. Not to make people feel good about themselves. To actually change them — systematically, measurably, and permanently.

---

## The Vision (5 Years)

Percentile.One is the world's most trusted personal transformation platform.

"Most trusted" means:
- Users know that a completion in P1 is real (Proof Engine)
- Users know that the agent tells them the truth (Honest Intelligence)
- Users know that the platform serves their growth, not engagement (Identity Before Achievement)
- Users recommend P1 to the people they care about most

By 2031:
- 1,000,000 active users running 12-week cycles
- Average 3+ cycles completed per user
- Verified proof system with <1% false completion rate
- Claude API powering every agent function
- Native iOS and Android applications
- Enterprise offering for high-performance teams and executive coaches
- Research partnership studying long-term behavioural change at scale

---

## The Core Metric

**Cycles Completed.**

Not daily active users. Not sessions per week. Not streak length.

**Cycles Completed.**

A completed 12-week cycle, with goals achieved and verified, represents 84 days of structured commitment. It is the most meaningful unit of transformation we can measure. Every product decision is optimised to increase the number of cycles completed per user per year.

Secondary metrics:
- Goal completion rate within a cycle (target: >60%)
- Proof submission rate (target: >85% when requested)
- Mid-cycle review completion rate (target: >75%)
- Vision alignment score across active users (target: >70%)

Anti-metrics (we do not optimise for):
- Session length
- Notifications sent
- Features clicked
- Streaks started (starts are easy; completions are meaningful)

---

## The Hierarchy of Value

From highest to lowest priority:

1. **Transformation** — Did the user actually change? Did their life improve?
2. **Integrity** — Is the platform honest? Are completions real?
3. **Insight** — Does the user understand themselves better?
4. **Execution** — Does the platform help them act on what they know?
5. **Experience** — Is the platform beautiful and delightful to use?

Beauty matters. Delight matters. But they serve transformation, not the other way around.

---

## What Success Looks Like

A user who has been on P1 for 18 months has:
- Completed 6 twelve-week cycles
- Achieved 70%+ of their cycle goals
- Built 3+ lasting habits verified by the platform
- Seen their trust score rise from 50 to 80+
- Connected their daily actions to a Vision they wrote on Day 1
- Become someone they are proud to be

That is the product we are building.

---

## What Failure Looks Like

A user who has been on P1 for 18 months has:
- Lots of streaks that broke at day 3
- Goals they created and never revisited
- A Today Screen full of cards they never open
- A trust score that never moved
- No vision, no cycle, no proof

This is the failure we must prevent. Every design decision, every engineering choice, every agent rule exists to pull users away from this outcome and toward the one above.

---

## The Question We Always Ask

Before shipping any feature, adding any screen, writing any agent output, or making any product decision:

> **Does this help the user become who they intend to become?**

If yes: build it.
If no: don't.
If unsure: wait.

The North Star is simple. Keep it visible.
