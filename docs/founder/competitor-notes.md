# Competitor notes

A running list of what we have actually seen of other interview-practice products. Each entry says who saw it, when, and how sure we are. **Only claim in public what was observed**, and never name or criticise a competitor in marketing: describe what we do instead.

## Seen by Francis directly

### InterviewPractice.com (16 Oct 2026)

- **What Francis did:** started a "Software Engineer interview" with an interviewer called "Michael AI" on `www.interviewpractice.com`, while signed in.
- **What the screen showed:** two tiles, one for the AI and one for the candidate, and a Stop Interview button. The AI tile was a grey circle with the broken-image text "user's profile image", the label "AI is thinking", and no face, lips or reaction.
- **Result:** still on "AI is thinking" after about half an hour. Francis ran it **three times and got the same result each time**, so this is repeatable from his account and browser, not a one-off.
- **Still unknown:** whether it also fails for other people, browsers or roles (a microphone or region issue on our side is unlikely after three identical runs, but not ruled out). If it matters, try a second role or browser and record the questions, voice, feedback, scoring and price.
- **How sure:** three identical runs by one person. Good enough for our own notes and for saying in conversation "I tried it three times and it never got past thinking". Not enough to publish a claim about the product.

### Top 3 data-science prep platforms (6 Oct 2026)

- Francis visited the top three from a Medium roundup and found online coding and SQL practice, with no spoken practice.
- The other four in that roundup, including Aced (formerly Exponent), are **not checked**. Aced is the one most likely to offer mock interviews, so check it before naming it.
- Safe line: "coding drills teach you to solve it; interviews test whether you can explain it out loud."

## From research, not yet seen by us

### LinkedIn Premium (researched Oct 2026, for the recruiter brief)

- Offers AI interview practice on job posts where the member is rated a strong match: role questions and written feedback on a transcript. Text based, behind a paid subscription.
- Francis has Premium and could not find it anywhere. Say "I couldn't find it", not "it doesn't exist".

### Indeed Career Scout (researched Oct 2026)

- Offers an AI mock-interview tool. Candidate side and self-serve. Not seen by us.

### PracticAI Interview Kits (seen in a LinkedIn post, 17 Oct 2026)

- **What the post says:** 13 role-specific "interview kits" for AI roles (AI engineer, RAG engineer, AI agents, MLOps and others), with 6,400+ answered interview questions, 3,900+ visuals and diagrams, real-world scenarios, system design and trade-off questions, and "questions-only" files for self-assessment.
- **What it is:** study material (questions and answers) offered through a link in the post. We did not see how it is sold, the price, or any spoken practice.
- **Lesson worth keeping:** its message is "don't prepare for every role, choose your target role", the same idea as our subject map by profession.
- **How sure:** from the vendor's own post only. We have not seen the product.

### What we have not found anywhere

- A live interviewer who speaks, reacts and scores, in the recruiter's hands to send with an interview booking. This is "as far as we can find", not a claim about the whole market.

## Questions people raise, and the answer

| Question | Answer to give |
|---|---|
| "Others do something similar" | Say what you have actually seen (see the entries above), then what ours does: a spoken interview with a live face that reacts, scored. Do not generalise about the whole market. |
