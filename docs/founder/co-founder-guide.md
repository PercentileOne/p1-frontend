## Equity — The % Question

- **50% is way too much** for someone coming in at this stage. That is a co-founder from day one split. You have already built the product, the vision, the brand, the tech.
- **10–20%** is more realistic for someone joining now as a strategic/design partner
- **30% maximum** as you said is already generous given where you are
- **Vesting schedule is non-negotiable** — any equity must vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff

### What is a Vesting Schedule?
- Equity is released over time, not all at once
- A **4-year vest with a 1-year cliff** means:
  - If he leaves after 6 months → he gets **nothing**
  - If he stays 1 year → he gets **25%** of his agreed share
  - Each month after that → a small additional portion releases
  - After 4 years → he has received 100% of his agreed share
- This protects you from someone taking equity and disappearing

## Funding Stages — A Quick Lesson

| Stage | What it means | Typical amount |
|---|---|---|
| **Bootstrapped** | You fund it yourself | £0 external |
| **Pre-seed** | Friends, angels, early believers | £50k–£500k |
| **Seed** | First real investors, product exists | £500k–£2M |
| **Series A** | Proven product, growing users, scaling | £2M–£15M |
| **Series B+** | Aggressive expansion | £15M+ |

You are currently **bootstrapped, approaching pre-seed.**

Series A is a few years away — but the equity decisions you make NOW determine how much of that you own when you get there. Every percentage point you give away today is multiplied many times over at Series A.

## Protecting Yourself — Key Legal Terms

Before signing anything, make sure you understand and have these in place:

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Vesting** | Equity released over time, not all at once |
| **Cliff** | Minimum time before any equity kicks in (usually 1 year) |
| **Founder's Agreement** | Simple legal document defining roles, equity, decision-making |
| **IP Assignment** | Everything built belongs to the company, not the individual |
| **Non-compete** | If he leaves, he cannot build a competing product |
| **Drag-along rights** | Majority shareholders can force minority to sell if there's an acquisition |
| **Step-in rights** | If a co-founder stops contributing, you can reduce their equity |

**Get a startup solicitor to draft the Founder's Agreement.** It costs a few hundred pounds and is worth every penny.

## Product Hunt — What It Is

Think of it as the **Oscars for new tech products.**

Every day, makers launch their products on producthunt.com. The community votes, comments, and shares. The top products get:
- Featured on the homepage
- In the newsletter (sent to 500,000+ tech enthusiasts, investors, journalists)

- Coverage by TechCrunch, The Verge, indie tech blogs
- Noticed by investors who watch Product Hunt specifically for opportunities
- "Product of the Day" badge — permanent credibility on your website
