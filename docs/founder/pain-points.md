# Pain points first

A working list for posts, adverts, landing pages and recruiter outreach. Started 15 October 2026 from Francis's reading: **say what hurts, in the reader's own words, before saying what we sell.** A speech therapist lists what parents worry about before offering anything.

## The shape

1. **The pain**, specific and in the reader's words ("You know your answers, but your mind goes blank when someone is watching.")
2. **The way out** (a rehearsal that feels real, scored, private).
3. **The offer** (try it free, no sign-up).

Rules we keep: true and specific, never shaming or frightening; always say it is private and that mistakes here do not matter ("Make your mistakes here, not in the real interview").

## Candidates

From Francis:
- Nerves
- A feeling of being unprepared
- Imposter syndrome
- Lack of confidence
- Uncomfortable talking, even to two people on a panel
- Not sure how their CV reads

Suggested additions (mine, to be kept or struck out by Francis):
- Blanking on a question you know the answer to
- Rambling, and not knowing when to stop
- Not knowing what they will ask
- Never having said the answers out loud
- Friends being too kind to tell you how you really sound
- Applying again and again and never hearing why
- Interviewing in a second language
- Going back to interviews after years out, or changing career

## Recruiters

From Francis:
- Candidates not passing the first interview stage
- Not making enough placements

Suggested additions (mine):
- A fee lost after weeks of sourcing, screening and submitting
- "Great on paper, poor in the room"
- Client feedback that says "did not interview well"
- Giving the same briefing again and again, with no way to rehearse twenty candidates
- Nothing to send with the interview confirmation but "good luck"

## Where it is already used, and where it is not (15 October 2026)

- **Pain first already:** the recruiter page's second section ("The gap"), slide 2 of the pitch deck ("The problem"), and the homepage's "Make your mistakes here" section.
- **Product first:** the homepage hero ("Practice interviewing with AI. Get interview coaching. Get that job.") and the native posts sent on 14 October. These are the places to try the shape above, one at a time, tagged so the funnel can compare.
