Questions

Quick answers.

Is the "Try it live" really free?

Yes. No account, no card. You name a subject, answer three questions, and get a scored result. If you like it, your first full interview is free too.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You can be interviewed on any job, company, exam or subject — from nursing and teaching to sales, finance and engineering.

Can I speak my answers?

Yes — answer with your microphone or type. Interviews are available in 32 languages.

What does it cost after the free interview?

£4.99 a month for up to 3 interviews a day and 10 a month, plus Learn, Career Coach and Careers. Or a one-off £5.99 seven-day pass. Cancel any time.

Who can see my interview?

You decide. You choose whether each interview stays private or is shared, and you can change it whenever you like.

I'm a recruiter or employer — how do I get in?
