The gap

The interview is the one moment that decides whether you earn anything.

You spend days sourcing, screening and submitting. Then the candidate gets a time, a place and “good luck”.

Briefing is not rehearsal

You send the job spec and have a call. But no one can have twenty candidates say their answers out loud to a listener, and get scored, the night before.

The cost lands on you

A candidate who fumbles the interview is a lost fee and weeks of work gone. You are the one who loses.

Nobody is equipped to help

Recruitment is built to find and submit. Preparing the candidate is nobody’s job and nobody’s tool. That is the step we add.

How it works

One link, in the message you are already sending.

1

You send a link

From the recruiter portal, with the job spec and the candidate’s CV loaded. It goes with the interview confirmation, so there is no extra step for them to find.

2

They rehearse for the real job

Up to three spoken practice interviews with a live interviewer who looks at them, listens and reacts, in 32 languages. Scored on five dimensions, with a path to a better pass.

3

They walk in ready

The result and certificate belong to the candidate, who can share it with you. Practice is private, so they can make their mistakes here, not in the real interview.

The habit is the whole idea: a prep link with every interview you arrange. It takes about a minute.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Does this replace my own briefing?

No. Your briefing tells them about the role and the client. This is the rehearsal that follows it: saying the answers out loud and hearing where they are weak.

What does the candidate have to do?

Open the link. There is nothing to install. They can type or speak their answers, on a phone or a computer.

Is the candidate’s practice private?

Yes. Nothing is public, and nothing goes to recruiters, unless the candidate chooses to share it. They can delete any interview, or their whole account, whenever they like.

Which jobs and languages?

Any job, company or subject, in 32 languages. The interview is built from the job spec and the CV you attach.

Will I see how they did?

The result belongs to the candidate, who can share it with you. A report for recruiters on what you sent and how it went is on our next-build list.
