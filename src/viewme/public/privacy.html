Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026
Plain-English summary: we collect what we need to run your practice interviews, coach you, and (if you're a recruiter or employer) help you find candidates — your profile, CV, and interview recordings — and we never sell your data. Some processing (AI scoring, text-to-speech, the live avatar) is handled by specialist providers on our behalf, under contract, never for their own purposes. Full detail below.

TheInterviewChair.com is operated by Percentile One Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England. This policy explains what personal data we collect across our platform (candidate, recruiter, and employer portals), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it, in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers everyone who uses TheInterviewChair.com in any role: candidates recording practice interviews, recruiters and employers searching for talent, and visitors to our marketing site.

2. What we collect

Account & profile information

  • Name, email address, and password (stored securely hashed — we never see or store your password in plain text)
  • Job title, job role, company, bio, phone number, location, and career preferences you choose to add to your profile
  • Profile photo and banner image, if you upload one

CV and career content

  • Your CV or résumé text, used to personalise your practice interview questions
  • The job title, job specification, and Special Focus topics you provide before an interview

Interview recordings and data

  • Video and audio recordings of your practice interviews, if you choose to record one
  • The questions asked, your spoken or typed answers, and the AI-generated scores and feedback for each session
  • Whether you've made a completed interview public or kept it private, and who you've shared it with

My Career Coach and Learn activity

  • Messages you send to My Career Coach, and its replies, stored so your conversation history is there when you come back
  • Course topics you study in the Learn engine

Recruiter and employer activity

  • Candidate searches, saved candidates, talent alerts you set up, and introductions you send
  • For accounts we set up directly (recruiters and employers don't self-register), the details you give us over the phone or by email to create your account

Payment information

When paid subscriptions launch, payment is handled entirely by our payment processor — we never see or store your full card details ourselves.

Technical and usage information

  • Standard web analytics (pages viewed, general location derived from IP address, device and browser type) via Google Analytics
  • Log data and error reports that help us keep the platform running reliably

4. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the specialist providers who help us run the platform, each acting under contract and only for the purposes we specify:

  • Microsoft Azure — hosts our databases, file storage, and AI processing infrastructure
  • Azure AI / OpenAI-compatible models — processes your interview answers and Career Coach messages to generate questions, scores, feedback, and replies
  • ElevenLabs — converts text into the spoken voice you hear from our interviewers and the Read Aloud feature
  • HeyGen (LiveAvatar) — powers the live video avatars during your interview session
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymised usage statistics for our marketing site
  • Web3Forms — delivers messages sent through our contact form

We may also disclose data where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Percentile One Ltd, our users, or the public.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and interview data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Children's privacy

TheInterviewChair.com is intended for people preparing for real job interviews and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

8. International data transfers

Some of our service providers process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to keep your data protected to UK standards.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage, access controls, and secure, private cloud storage with time-limited access links for video and audio content. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes, we'll let you know directly.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at francis@percentile.one, or via our contact page.
