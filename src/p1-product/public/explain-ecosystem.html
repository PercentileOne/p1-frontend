HOW CANDIDATES DISCOVER EXPLAIN

Three gateways into
the ecosystem

Every candidate journey begins at one of three entry points. Each one drops the candidate directly into the right experience — no friction, no login, no setup.

01

Jobs Board → Magic Button

A candidate sees a job posting — on LinkedIn, a company careers page, or the Explain Jobs Board. They click "Prep for Interview" and land directly inside Interview Prep, pre-loaded with the role and company.

Questions are generated for their exact role — no form-filling, no account. Thirty seconds from click to first question.

INSTANT PREP ROLE PRE-LOADED
02

Social Media → Self-Serve Learning

A candidate arrives at candidate.explain.global via a TikTok ad, Instagram story, or organic search. They enter the Candidate Home — a self-serve hub where they choose their own path.

They enter the role, paste their CV, choose 3 or 6 questions, and build their own prep pack in under a minute. No recruiter involvement required.

SELF-SERVE SOCIAL-FIRST
03

Recruiter → Candidate Magic Link

A recruiter has placed a candidate for interview. From the Interview Intake, they click "Send Prep Link →" — this generates a personalised, hash-encoded URL containing the candidate's questions, role, company, and context.

The candidate clicks the link and lands inside their own tailored prep pack. They also receive feedback and LEARN recommendations via a separate link after the interview.

PORTAL ONE

The Recruiter Portal recruiter.explain.global

The recruiter's command centre. Everything from CV parsing to question generation, AI voice interviewing, client briefing, and sending feedback to candidates — all in one portal, all in one session.

1
Upload a CV and job spec. The AI parses both in seconds. A structured candidate profile is built automatically — roles, skills, experience, seniority, achievements.
2
Questions are generated. 6 role-specific questions + 2 HR questions — tailored to the exact role, company, industry, and candidate background. Not generic templates.
3
The interview runs. Two AI interviewers — Sarah (HR) and James (specialist, title inferred from role) — conduct the session with voice. Answers are transcribed and scored in real time.
4
Send to Client or Candidate. One click generates a private, hash-encoded link for the hiring manager (client brief) or the candidate (prep pack). No backend. No login required.

CLIENT

PORTAL TWO

The Client Portal client.explain.global

The hiring manager's private briefing room. A link arrives from the recruiter — no login, no account, no app install. The client opens it, reads the candidate profile, studies the questions, and provides feedback — all in one confidential URL.

1
Interview Overview. Stat cards show role questions vs culture fit questions, total count, and the candidate's years of experience. Recommended interview flow with timing. Skills to probe. Interview tips.
2
Questions & Follow-Ups. Every question shown with source tag (Role / HR), difficulty, competency. Expand any card to see the model answer guide, follow-up probes (↳), and red flags to watch for.
3
Candidate Profile. Work history, skills, education, and achievements — all parsed from the CV. Seniority, years of experience, current role and company prominently surfaced.
4
Provide Feedback. 4-step guided flow: select outcome (Pass / Leave Door Open / Not Progressing), select improvement chips, add notes, and AI generates a kind, professional 3-paragraph feedback summary. Copy and send to recruiter.
